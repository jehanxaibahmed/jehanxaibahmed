## 404? Not here. You found me! <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jehanxaibahmed/jehanxaibahmed/main/wave.gif" width="30">

I'm a **Full Stack Engineer** with 7+ years of experience building scalable web and mobile applications, and I now focus on **production AI systems** that automate real business operations. I work across .NET Core, Vue, Node.js and PostgreSQL on Google Cloud and Azure, with a background in enterprise blockchain.

🏢 **Currently** a Senior Web Developer at **Nation Wilcox** in Birmingham, UK, working on Caterpoint (online ordering), ISAAC (wholesale and food-distribution ERP) and an AI order-ingestion platform.

🤖 **Building with AI:** LLM pipelines that turn customer emails, voicemails and supplier invoices into structured orders and invoices, using multi-model routing, embeddings and vector search, speech-to-text, consensus voting and guardrails.

🧠 **Previously** built blockchain platforms InvoiceMate and DocsMate at MATESOL: Hyperledger Fabric networks, chaincode and Kubernetes deployments.

💬 **Ask me about:** applied AI, order-to-cash systems, .NET, Vue and blockchain.
